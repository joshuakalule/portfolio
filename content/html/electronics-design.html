I also engage in the design and development of functional electronic prototypes for healthcare, utilities, and various other projects.

Research & Planning

Research, identify and define the project requirements, constraints, and objectives to guide the development of the project.

Component Selection

Selecting suitable microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and power supplies, ensuring compatibility and availability.

Schematic design

Create schematic diagram using software tools to show connection between components.

Simulation and Testing

Simulate the circuit using software tools and build a prototype on a breadboard to identify potential issues.

Firmware development

Writing and testing the firmware or software to run on the microcontroller using arduino or python.

Assembly and soldering

Assembling components on a PCB and soldering them in place.

Final assembly and enclosure

Assembling the final product, including any enclosures or housings and ensuring the project is safe, reliable, and aesthetically pleasing.
